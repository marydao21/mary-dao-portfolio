01 · DATA & WELLBEINGJul 2026 · Team

The Prosperity Paradox

Overview:Interactive analysis asking whether Australia’s material living standards improved while social and emotional wellbeing deteriorated between 2010 and 2024.

Impact:Shows why income and employment alone provide an incomplete picture of national progress, using peer-country comparisons and transparent sensitivity checks.

Description:Compares Australia with eight peer countries across income, employment, housing, health, social connection and subjective wellbeing, delivered through a Shiny dashboard, Quarto report and presentation.

Technology: R, Shiny, Quarto, tidyverse, Plotly, OECD data.

My role: Co-author and team member with Damian Nguyen; contributed to the analysis and final project outputs.

Why me

What is it like working with me?

I bring analytical discipline, commercial curiosity and a collaborative way of working.

Curious

I ask what is driving the result, not only what the number says. That curiosity helps me understand unfamiliar systems, investigate data carefully and find useful connections.

Proactive

I clarify requirements early, plan the work and keep momentum rather than waiting for every next step. When I see a gap, I try to bring a practical option to the team.

Collaborative

I communicate clearly, listen to different perspectives and translate between technical and business viewpoints. I want teammates to understand the reasoning, not just receive an answer.

Accountable

I take responsibility for the quality of my work, follow through on commitments and communicate honestly when assumptions, limitations or risks need attention.
